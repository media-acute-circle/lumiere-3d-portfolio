Eight days to launch day

A focused MVP, built around the store opening. Here is who does what, and what we need from you to start.

  6. 20 Oct
    Launch on store opening dayBoth

The main risk is photos and specifications. If they arrive after 14 October, we launch with fewer products and add the rest in the first weeks.

What we need from you

  1. The 5 to 7 pieces you most want online first, with names and codes.
  2. Photos of each piece, and any existing 3D files or CAD drawings.
  3. Specifications: size, wattage, lumens, colour temperature, IP rating and warranty.
  4. The person who will answer quote requests, their phone number, and how fast they can reply.
  5. Your logo, and any brand colours or fonts.

Also name the two or three people who will use the admin dashboard. If anything is not ready, tell us and we will work around it.
