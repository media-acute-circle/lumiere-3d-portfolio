## 2. What the reference sites are missing

We read seven reference sites. Most of them sell lighting well, but they
leave the buyer to do the hard work. The table shows the gaps we saw and
what our website adds in each case.

| Gap we saw | Where | What we add | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| No full specification on product pages | Most product listings | A specification tab with wattage, lumens, colour temperature, IP rating and size | The buyer can compare without calling |
| No close-up of finish | Most sites | Zoom and 360° view with finish options | The buyer sees the craft from home |
| No light-on view | All seven | A lit photo now, an interactive preview later | Shows what the fixture does with the light on |
| Unclear next step after an enquiry | Most sites | A quote form that says when the buyer will hear back | Removes doubt |
| Installation and warranty missing | Pages read | An install note and warranty on every product | Answers the questions architects ask first |
| Little project proof | Most sites | Project pages with credited designers | Builds trust with trade buyers |
| Prices hidden with no reason | Several sites | Price on request, with a short reason and response time | Honest, and sets the right expectation |
| Showroom tours do not link to products | White Teak | Hotspots that open the product (later phase) | Turns a visit into a product view |

**What this makes possible:** a product page that answers what a
salesperson would answer — what it is made of, how big it is, what light it
gives, what it needs to install, and how to ask for a price. A buyer who
gets those answers arrives at the quote form ready to buy.

> **Caveat.** These gaps come from the pages we read. We did not open every
> product page on every site, so we will confirm them before the design
> review.

## 4. Admin dashboard

Your team manages the website from **its own dashboard, built for you**.
There is no WordPress or third-party content system to learn, and no
separate CMS licence to pay for. Small changes — a product name, a
description or a price — take a few minutes and never wait for us.

### 4.1 What the team can do in the MVP

- **Products.** Add a product, change its name and description, upload and
  reorder photos, set the main photo, and update the price or switch it to
  *"Price on request"*.
- **Specifications.** Edit size, wattage, lumens, colour temperature, IP
  rating, warranty and installation note for each product.
- **Visibility.** Publish, hide or feature a product on the home page.
- **Categories.** Add, rename and reorder categories, and assign products to
  them.
- **Enquiries.** See every quote request and store visit booking in one
  inbox. Mark each one as *new*, *contacted* or *closed*, and add notes.
- **Basic settings.** Contact details, WhatsApp number, opening hours and a
  banner for announcements such as the store opening.

**Every enquiry goes into the dashboard the moment it is submitted.** The
owner also gets an email alert, so no lead depends on someone checking a
mailbox.

### 4.2 Roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access, including prices and users |
| **Editor** | Edits products and content and handles enquiries; cannot change prices or add users |

### 4.3 Dashboard roadmap

| Stage | What the team gets |
|---|---|
| **MVP** | Products, specifications, photos, categories, enquiry inbox, basic settings |
| **Post-MVP** | Bulk price updates from a spreadsheet, project page editor, per-page search settings, a simple visitor report |
| **Further phases** | AI writing assistant for descriptions, marketing asset generator, social media scheduling and posting, WhatsApp campaigns, trade accounts |

## 6. Delivery phases

### Phase 1 — MVP, live by 20 October

**Goal:** a working website on launch day that lets visitors find a piece,
understand it, and ask for a quote.

- Nine public pages from §3, plus the admin dashboard.
- 10 to 15 products with photos, specifications and a quote button.
- Zoom and 360° photos for every product; a 3D viewer only where a model
  exists.
- A quote form that sends each enquiry to your team by email, with a
  WhatsApp option.
- Basic analytics, so we can see visits and enquiries from the first day.
- Tested on Android and iPhone, on mobile data.

**Not in Phase 1:** the full catalogue, the 3D showroom tour, augmented
reality, and the trade page.

### Phase 2 — Post-MVP, the first months after launch

Timing depends on what the first few weeks of enquiries show. We will agree
the dates in the Phase 1 review.

- Full product range added in batches, with photos and specifications.
- Projects page with credited designers and architects.
- Architects and designers page, with specification downloads and a project
  brief form.
- Compare tool for two or three products.
- Light-on photos for every product that has them.
- 3D viewers added to products as their models arrive.
- A short care and installation guide for each product category.
- Dashboard upgrades: bulk price updates from a spreadsheet, a project page
  editor and a simple visitor report.

### Phase 3 — Further phases

These need 3D models, budget and enquiry data. We will decide the order once
Phase 2 is running.

- 3D tour of the showroom, with hotspots that open each product.
- Light-on preview with dimming, and colour temperature where the data is
  verified.
- Augmented reality placement for products with real-scale models.
- Room scenes showing a fixture in a typical interior.
- A trade login for dealers and architects, with pricing where agreed.
- A simple product configurator for pieces with finish or size choices.
- AI writing assistant, marketing asset generator and a social media panel
  inside the admin dashboard.

## 8. What we need from you

1. **Launch pieces.** The 10 to 15 pieces you most want online first, with
   their names and codes.
2. **Media.** Photos of each piece, and any existing 3D files or CAD
   drawings.
3. **Specifications** for each piece: size, wattage, lumens, colour
   temperature, IP rating and warranty.
4. **Quote owner.** The name and phone number of the person who will answer
   quote requests, and how quickly they can reply.
5. **Brand assets.** Your logo and any brand colours or fonts, if you have
   them.

Also name the **two or three people on your team who will use the admin
dashboard**. If some of these are not ready, tell us and we will work around
them. **Missing specifications are hidden, not guessed.**
